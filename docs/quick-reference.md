# Quick Reference

One-page summary of the most important rules. Print this or keep it on your phone.

---

## Vehicle Requirements

| Item                    | Rule                                      |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Engine                  | 4-cylinder only (no rotary)               |
| Drivetrain              | 2-wheel-drive only                        |
| Tires                   | Unaltered street-tread only               |
| Brake light             | At least one working                      |
| Helmet                  | DOT approved with chin strap              |
| Fire extinguisher       | 1 in car + 1 in pit                       |
| Mud flaps               | Required on all RWD vehicles at start     |
| Bumpers                 | Stock or limited fabricated (see Technical) |

---

## Team Requirements

| Item                    | Rule                                      |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Drivers                 | Minimum 4 licensed drivers                |
| Under 18                | Parent/guardian consent required          |
| RACEceiver              | Required + must respond                   |
| Transponders            | 2 working units per car ($20/set)         |
| Driver changes          | Mandatory when instructed                 |
| Multiple shifts         | Not allowed in the same car               |
| Registration            | $100 by deadline / $50 late fee           |

---

## Safety Highlights

- **Red flag** → Stop immediately
- **Black flag** → Exit to pits immediately
- Mandatory pit stop for flat tire or causing a red flag
- **No kids in the pits**
- **No weapons of any kind**
- Head of Safety decisions are final

---

## Claims

- Amount: **$1,500**
- Deadline: End of the **50th lap**
- Must be cash in hand to Safety Coordinator
- Deny a claim → car is banned for life
