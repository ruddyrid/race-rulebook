# Race Rules Overview

All teams must have four individual drivers for the A Main. The idea behind this race is not one of the smartest we've had. Whether you're racing or watching, you're doing so at your own risk.

## Key Requirements at a Glance

| Requirement              | Details                                      |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Drivers per team         | Minimum of 4 licensed drivers                |
| Vehicle type             | 4-cylinder, 2-wheel-drive only               |
| Helmet                   | DOT approved with chin strap                 |
| Fire extinguisher        | 1 in car + 1 in pit                          |
| Transponders             | 2 working units per car                      |
| RACEceiver               | Required and must respond to communications  |
| Registration             | $100 by deadline, $50 late fee after         |
