# Vehicle Classes

## Cars & Trucks

1. 4-cylinder vehicles only. No rotary-type engines.
2. 2-wheel-drive vehicles only.
3. You must have at least one working brake light when the race starts.
4. A seat belt, door, and helmet are required for everyone riding in the car. Helmets must be DOT approved with a chin strap. No batting helmets, bicycle helmets, or hard hats; Those are not legal headgear.
5. Bumpers. See [bumper rules](#bumper-rules) below.
6. One fire extinguisher in every car, and one in the pit for every team.
7. Unaltered street-tread tires only. **No mud terrain, no all terrain, no snow tires.**
8. All rear-wheel-drive vehicles must start the race with mud flaps.

!!! note "Simple Summary"
    4-cylinder, 2WD, street tires, DOT helmet, working brake light, fire extinguisher, and legal bumpers. That’s the core of it.
